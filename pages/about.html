About Me

I am a beginner developer learning computer science by building real projects. My goal is to become a full-stack developer and eventually a technical founder.

Instead of waiting until I know everything, I created Project CS to document the process of learning, experimenting, making mistakes, and improving over time.

Why I Built Project CS

Project CS is my first major development project. It began as a website for documenting what I learn, but it will continue evolving as my skills and projects grow.

The website is intentionally playful and game-inspired because it represents progress rather than a finished professional portfolio.

How the Journey Works

World
A major area of computer science or development that I am currently learning, such as Web Development or Python.
Project
Something I build to apply the skills learned inside a programming world.
Quest
The specific task or milestone that I am currently working toward.
Achievement
A meaningful skill or development milestone that I have completed.

Long-Term Goal

My long-term goal is to become capable of designing, building, and launching complete software products. Project CS will document the projects and skills that move me toward becoming a full-stack developer and technical founder.
